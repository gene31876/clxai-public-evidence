Status: **PLANNING UPDATE ONLY — NO TOKEN TRANSFER AUTHORIZED**

## Updated fixed pilot limits

The planned first CLXAI community Airdrop pilot is prepared with the following
fixed limits:

- exactly **10,000 CLXAI per selected wallet**;
- no more than **100 selected wallets**;
- no more than **1,000,000 CLXAI in total**.

The source remains the `community-rewards` allocation. The maximum total equals,
but does not exceed, the existing Treasury maximum single release.

## Schedule and access remain unchanged

The eligibility snapshot remains **29 August 2026 at 16:00 UTC**
(18:00 Europe/Berlin). Address submission remains closed. Its opening time, if
approved, will be communicated before submissions begin.

## No value or allocation promise

The higher token amount is a fixed pilot-policy limit, not a statement about
price or market value. Participation does not guarantee selection or an
allocation. No purchase, payment, token holding, referral, or financial
contribution is required.

## Distribution remains controlled

This update does not authorize a token transfer, Treasury release, Squads
proposal, signature, or distribution. Any later distribution remains subject
to final candidate validation, independent technical review, a separate owner
approval for the exact reviewed files, a reviewed Treasury release, and Squads
2-of-3 approval.
